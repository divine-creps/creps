CREPS Hauts-de-France

The only centre for performance north of Paris.

Located in the heart of the Lille European metropolis, CREPS Hauts-de-France is a sports preparation and improvement centre for high-level athletes and young talents.

Member of the Grand INSEP network and positioned as an Olympic & Paralympic training centre, the CREPS brings together athletes, coaches, medical experts and performance specialists in one environment.
